How it works

Four steps from the walkaround to a delivered PDF.

  1. 01

    Decode the VIN

    Type the 17-digit VIN and the year, make and model arrive from the NHTSA database. Add mileage, plate and colors and the identification page is done.

  2. 02

    Work the checklist

    Work through the sections, rate the condition grid and leave comments where they matter — one thumb on a phone, or a keyboard on a laptop.

  3. 03

    Shoot the photos

    Straight from the camera or the file picker, captioned and compressed on the way in, so the finished file stays small enough to text.

  4. 04

    Send the report

    The PDF is built on the spot and goes out by text, email or share link before you pull off the lot. The customer opens it and understands it — no follow-up call to walk them through it.

Half-finished inspections save as drafts, so a garage with no signal is not a lost job. Pick it back up when you have bars.

FAQ

Questions we get every week

Is this a subscription? +

No. $499 once, and the platform is yours. There is no monthly fee, no per-report fee and no seat licensing — put as many inspectors on it as you like.

Does anyone need to install an app? +

No. It runs in the mobile browser on iOS and Android and on desktop, so there is nothing to download from an app store and nothing to update on each phone when something changes.

Can I change the checklist? +

Yes. Add, rename or delete any item, drag items between sections, reorder whole sections, and reset to the factory list whenever you want to start clean. Anything you leave unmarked is left off the printed report, so the document only shows what you actually looked at.

Can I put my own company on the reports? +

Yes — logo, business name, address, phone, website, disclaimer and an About page in your own words, on every page of every report. The checklist is yours to rewrite too, so the document reads like your process rather than someone else's template.

Where do the finished reports live? +

In your own Google Drive, logged to your own Google Sheet, under your own login. From the success screen you can send the PDF by text, email or share link straight away.

What if the vehicle has no cell signal? +

Work is saved as a draft you can resume, so a bad-signal garage is not a lost inspection. Decoding a VIN and generating the PDF both need a connection.

Do I have to state a value on a report? +

No. A pre-purchase inspection carries no figure at all unless you add one, and on appraisal types you can leave the value blank so nothing prints. Total loss reports are the exception in the other direction: they state a single actual cash value, because that is what a carrier settles on.
